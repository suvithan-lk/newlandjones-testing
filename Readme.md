# Newland Jones Website

Modern, professional and conversion-focused website for **Newland Jones**, a UK accounting firm.

The website is designed to communicate the firm's accounting, tax and business advisory services in a clear, modern and approachable way while providing a strong path for potential clients to make an enquiry or book a consultation.

---

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Name

**Newland Jones Website**

### Project Type

Corporate / Professional Services Website

### Business Category

Accounting & Business Advisory

### Target Market

United Kingdom

### Primary Audience

* Sole traders
* Startups
* Small businesses
* Growing SMEs
* Established businesses
* Business owners seeking accounting, tax and advisory support

---

## 2. Project Objective

The primary objective of this website is to create a modern digital presence for Newland Jones that:

* Clearly explains the company's services
* Builds trust with potential clients
* Communicates the firm's approach
* Makes services easy to discover
* Generates consultation enquiries
* Supports organic search visibility
* Provides a scalable content structure
* Works effectively across desktop, tablet and mobile devices

---

## 3. Core Business Positioning

The website should communicate Newland Jones as a modern and approachable accounting firm focused on:

* Clear communication
* Proactive advice
* Real-time financial understanding
* Personal client relationships
* Transparent service
* Business growth support

The website should avoid presenting accounting as purely a compliance function.

The messaging should position Newland Jones as a business partner that helps clients understand their numbers and make informed business decisions.

---

## 4. Important Content Rule

Newland Jones content must be original.

BBK Partnership may be used as a reference for:

* Information architecture
* Page hierarchy
* Service organisation
* Professional-services website patterns
* User experience ideas

However, the following must **not** be copied:

* Written content
* Statistics
* Testimonials
* Team information
* Branding
* Visual identity
* Service descriptions
* Images
* Marketing claims

Newland Jones must have its own brand, voice and content.

# 11. UX Principles

The website should follow these principles:

### Clarity

Visitors should understand the company's offering quickly.

### Simplicity

Navigation and page structure should remain easy to understand.

### Trust

Professional credentials, team information, testimonials and useful content should establish credibility.

### Conversion

Every important page should provide a clear next step.

### Accessibility

The website should be usable by visitors with different abilities and input methods.

### Performance

Images, JavaScript and third-party resources should be optimised.


