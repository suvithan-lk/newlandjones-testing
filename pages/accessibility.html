Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want this site to be usable by everyone, regardless of ability or technology.

Our commitment

We aim to follow WCAG 2.2 AA-level practices. This site is built with semantic HTML, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, descriptive alt text and colour contrast kept in mind throughout.

What that means in practice

  • The full menu works with keyboard only — Tab, arrow keys, Enter and Escape
  • Menus announce their state with ARIA attributes
  • Forms label every field and announce errors to screen readers
  • Animations respect the reduced-motion system preference
  • Text scales with browser zoom without breaking layout

Known limitations

Some placeholder imagery and article cards are awaiting final content; where real content is not yet supplied, we've used text descriptions rather than empty visuals. These will be replaced as content is approved.

Reporting a problem

If anything on this site is difficult to use, tell us via the contact page and we'll fix it. Accessibility issues are treated as bugs, not feedback.

Enforcement

If you're not happy with our response, you can escalate to the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
